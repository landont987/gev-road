# Bug hunt 2026-10-20

Goal: find and fix as many bugs as possible before the first head-unit test (#1).
Every finding lists what was wrong, how it was found, where, whether it was fixed, and the version.

**Result:** 16 bugs fixed in 0.3.10 (`~/Desktop/GEV-Road-0.3.10.apk`, tag `v0.3.10`). Tests went from 132 to 165.

## Baseline (main at d8907ba, version 0.3.9)
- `npm test`: 132 tests, 132 pass.
- `sh scripts/test-java.sh`: ALL PASS (LineFramer only).

## How the hunt was done
- Read every `web/src/*.js` module, `index.html`, `style.css` and every Java file.
- The build targets `chrome61` with esbuild, so syntax (optional chaining, `??`, `**`, spread) is transpiled. A grep for newer APIs found none (`flat`, `fromEntries`, `allSettled`, `replaceAll`, `structuredClone`, `globalThis`, `queueMicrotask`, `matchAll`, `ResizeObserver`, `replaceChildren`, `append`, `closest`).
- Ran a TLS check of every outside host with TLS 1.2 as the ceiling (what Android 9 can do), using node `tls.connect({ maxVersion: 'TLSv1.2' })`. Only routing.openstreetmap.de (already handled) and **overpass-api.de** need TLS 1.3.
- Browser preview at 1280x720 (port 4180): map, demo drive, cameras and pictures, road cards, low data, radio sheet, voice panel, menu, drive mode. The console showed no errors besides Overpass 502s.
- Android 9 emulator (WebView 66, 1280x720, density 160) with a debug build and a CDP bridge to both WebViews:
  - map with 644 cameras, 317 road dots and 193 plate readers
  - radio drawer with play
  - a simulated drive with `adb emu geo fix` (crash cards and camera alerts spoke)
  - AI truck check, automatic and CHECK NOW
  - live calls: a 30 s check on the DPS pack, which signed in and listened
  - detector panel ("no USB host support" on the emulator), engine panel, low data
- Every testable bug got a failing node:test first. The new helpers `test/helpers/dom.mjs` and `fake-leaflet.mjs` let page modules run under node.

## Decisions (made without the owner, written down)
- The preview server needs `.claude/launch.json`. It is kept out of git with `.git/info/exclude`.
- Tests that import JSON register a node load hook (`registerHooks`) instead of changing the data file.
- **Radio drawer and Broadcastify's page:** the drawer script only scrolls their page to the player. It also presses their own × on the "Squawster" app banner (the site remembers that for 90 days) and gives their sign-in box the `top/right/bottom/left: 0` that their `inset: 0` means. No ads are removed, and the audio stays in their player (their terms).
- **AI truck check:** it was changed to two Gemini looks, because measured false positives would be spoken. Gemini is still free tier; a second request is sent only when a plausible vehicle is in view.
- The OpenAI voice timeout (6 s, on purpose) was left alone. It timed out once on the emulator. Watch it on the truck.
- Live calls were tested for 30 s only (billed per record).

## Still broken or not checked, and why
- **Most Phoenix ADOT cameras were offline** on 2026-10-20: AZ511 serves its 540x330 "no live feed" card for 21 of 26 sampled cameras, in browsers too. This is ADOT's side, not the app. The app says "This camera is offline" and the truck check skips them.
- **Overpass is flaky:** 502s, 406s for browser user agents, and 33 s answers. The bundled list stands in until a refresh works.
- **OpenAI voice** timed out once at its 6 s cap on the emulator (emulator network). Left alone on purpose; watch it on the truck. The emulator has no TTS engine, so built-in voice fallback was not heard.
- **Not tested here:** real GPS bearing (the emulator always reports 0), USB detector (no USB host on the emulator), AndrOBD readings, TomTom traffic (no key), performance on the MT8163 (#10), Broadcastify feed audio after sign-in (no password entered by Claude).
- **Not changed:** road dots are rebuilt as about 315 DOM markers on every feed refresh, and 644 camera markers stay in the DOM. This is a performance matter for #10, not a bug.
- **Unverified data:** the live-calls pack labels "Chandler Police" (2508-2498, 2508-2485) and "Avondale Police" (2508-4499) could not be rechecked against the coverage page (it loads dynamically).
